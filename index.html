Privacy policy — Ludo Blaze

Last updated: 5 October 2026
Applies to the Android game Ludo Blaze (app.ludoblaze.ludo).

Ludo Blaze (“the app”) is an offline board game for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information, including the advertising that supports it.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of ours. Your matches, settings and coins stay on your phone.
  • The only third party in the app is Google’s advertising SDK (AdMob). It collects the data Google needs to serve, measure and protect ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires consent — the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and several US states — the app asks with Google’s consent form before ads load, and keeps a way back to change your choice: Settings → Ad privacy options.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following in Android’s private app storage. It never leaves the phone and we never see it:

  • the match in progress, so you can resume it;
  • your settings (sound, music, haptics);
  • your earned coins and gems;
  • bookkeeping for ad frequency caps, and the Fair Play page’s record of your last match.

Uninstalling the app — or clearing its data in Android settings — deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

What the advertising SDK collects

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device’s general location;
  • User product interactions, including app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit, and that it is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google’s own policies govern what Google does with it: see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive your ad data; Google delivers the ads. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no social sign-in and no purchase system — there is nothing in it that reports to us.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent form. If you are in a region that requires consent, the app asks before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads). Deleting it does not remove ads; it stops them being personalised with that ID.
  • Rewarded ads are always opt-in — a video only plays if you tap the offer, and skipping it costs nothing.
  • Ads and the match. Ads never cover the board, the die or a move; an interstitial can appear only between matches.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It asks for no age, name or other personal detail, and it has no accounts or chat. If you believe a child under 13 has somehow provided personal information through the app, contact us at neeleshasati118@gmail.com and we will look into it; in practice nothing in the app transmits personal information to us at all.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. There is no server-side copy.
  • Ad data is held by Google under Google’s policies; the advertising ID behind it can be reset or deleted from Android settings at any time.
  • We keep no user records of our own, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — but a request to neeleshasati118@gmail.com will always be answered.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes what is collected — new ad formats, accounts, purchases — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. Where the law requires it, the app will ask for consent again.
